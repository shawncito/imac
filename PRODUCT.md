# Instituto Misionero — Palabra de Dios

## Product Purpose
Mobile-first PWA for a missionary institute. Displays Bible verses in 14 languages with text-to-speech playback. Used by missionaries, students, and church members worldwide as a devotional and outreach tool.

## Users
Missionaries and church members. Range from tech-novice to tech-savvy. Uses: personal devotion, sharing verses with contacts who speak different languages, evangelism.

## Register
product

## Brand
- **Name**: Instituto Misionero
- **Tone**: serious, reverent, trustworthy, international
- **Colors**: deep navy/midnight blue, warm gold accent (#c9a84c / #f0c040)
- **Anti-references**: flashy megachurch apps, neon televangelism, generic Bible app beige

## Strategic Principles
1. One tap to verse in any language
2. Audio must feel like listening to a real person, not a robot
3. Dark UI — used in churches, dimly lit settings, evening devotionals
4. Gold accent is sacred, not decorative. Use sparingly.
5. No clutter. The Word is the hero.
